Stadium & Ticket Guidelines

Ticket Verification

Keep your ticket or booking confirmation ready for verification at the stadium entrance. Use tickets only for the match, date, and venue shown on the booking.

ID Proof Requirement

Carry a valid government-issued photo ID. The name and details on your ID may be checked against your ticket or booking information.

Prohibited Items

Do not bring weapons, fireworks, outside alcohol, glass containers, or other items prohibited by the venue. Check the stadium’s official rules before attending, as restrictions may vary.
